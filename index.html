AI Conference Deadlines

Live countdowns to submission deadlines for AI conferences. Times are converted to your timezone (); each deadline is checked against the conference's official site.

Data merged from ccfddl and huggingface/ai-deadlines, then checked on each official site. ✓ on official site means the date appears on the conference's own page; ○ not confirmed means it didn't (the page may list it elsewhere). Always double-check the CFP before you submit. Refresh with uv run scripts/update.py.
